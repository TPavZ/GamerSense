GamerSense v0.4.14 - approve or delete

GET STARTED
Extract the complete ZIP into a new folder. App ZIP: open GamerSense.exe.
Source ZIP: run BUILD-DEBUG.bat, then open the Debug executable.
The app requires .NET 9 Desktop Runtime; building requires .NET 9 SDK.

KEEP YOUR WORKING PLAYBACK SETTINGS
Mode: Direct cable capture (test)
Game audio device: CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)
True output: Speakers (C6)
Game output: CABLE Input
Windows Listen to this device: OFF
Your existing selections and autosave choice are remembered.

REVIEW AFTER PLAYING
1. Stop GamerSense playback and open Event review.
2. Select a saved clip. Click Replay range to hear it.
3. Choose its sound tag and optional notes; adjust the range if needed.
4. Approve & export writes the selected range as WAV plus a verified JSON
   label to Documents/GamerSense/ApprovedClips, then removes the temporary
   source from the review queue. Approval means useful training sample.
5. Delete clip discards an unwanted clip after confirmation.
There is no Save tags or Save loaded clip for later button. No separate
Keep/Reduce choice is required. Approval is not a playback-volume instruction.

EXPORT FOLDER
The selected destination appears above the saved list. Choose export folder
changes it and remembers your choice. Open approved folder shows the exports.
Both WAV and JSON must be exported before the source is removed. If export
fails, the source remains available. Existing exports are not overwritten.
Clips from the old Approved list that still exist are also shown in the queue;
approve them again to export and remove them using this workflow.

RETENTION
Unexported clips still expire one hour after their original save time.
Cleanup runs about every 30 seconds while open, before saving, and at startup.
At the 2 GB temporary audio limit, new saves pause until space becomes free.
Approved exports are outside the temporary library and do NOT expire.
The exports folder can grow beyond 2 GB; that limit applies to the review queue.

Automatic spikes and manual marks still save 2 seconds before/3 seconds after.
Use Ctrl+Alt+F8 globally, or F8 while the app is focused, for manual marks.
The live timeline/sample tools remain available under their checkbox. Loaded
offline samples may still be replayed, but the approve/delete queue operates
on automatically saved live clips.
