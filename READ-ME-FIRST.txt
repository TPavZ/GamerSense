GamerSense v0.4.15 - select and repeat a sound event

GET STARTED
Extract the entire App ZIP into a NEW folder, then open GamerSense.exe.
Requires the .NET 9 Desktop Runtime, as before. Your saved audio-device,
mode and export-folder settings are retained. Stop the older app first.
Source ZIP: run BUILD-DEBUG.bat and open the resulting Debug executable.
Building requires the .NET 9 SDK. BUILD-DEBUG.bat is unchanged.

REVIEW NEW SAVED EVENTS
1. Stop game playback in GamerSense and open Event review.
2. Select a saved clip. The waveform shows the WHOLE clip.
3. Drag across the waveform to highlight just the sound you want to label.
   Drag either gold boundary to refine it, or type start/end seconds.
   Whole clip restores the complete range. The dashed line marks the
   detected spike, which may not be the exact sound onset.
4. Check Repeat selection, then click Replay range to listen repeatedly.
   Stop replay ends it. Changing the selection stops replay; click Replay
   range again to hear the revised section. Uncheck Repeat to finish the
   current pass. Review audio uses your selected true output device.
5. Choose a label and describe overlap/distance/type in the notes.
6. Approve & export writes ONLY the selected native audio frames plus the
   JSON label to your approved folder, then removes the pending clip.
   Use Delete clip for an unwanted pending sample, after confirmation.

REFINE YOUR EXISTING AC1 SAMPLES
Click Open WAV for review and choose a WAV from your AC1 folder.
The matching JSON label and notes load automatically when present.
Drag/select/repeat to focus on the blast onset and its audible tail.
Approve & export creates a NEW WAV + JSON pair. It keeps the original WAV
and JSON intact, even when exporting into the same approved folder.
Close sample leaves the original untouched. Reopen the original if you
want to export another section with a different label.

Separate grenade pin/throw sounds and detonator activation from the blast.
New labels include grenade handling / throw, detonator activation and
chambering. Mixed / uncertain remains available for overlapping sounds.
Do not label all five seconds as an explosion if the blast is much shorter.
Selection means the target sound is present; it does not prove other
categories are absent. Approval means useful training sample, not a request
to change that category's volume.

WAV imports support PCM8/16/24/32 and float32, including extensible PCM/float,
up to 60 seconds. Original channels/sample rate and selected bytes are kept.
Labels include selection offsets in the original clip, original session
bounds, spike timing and the imported parent filename when applicable.
Existing exported metadata remains compatible.

PLAYBACK AND STORAGE
The live capture/output path and timing profiles are unchanged. The loop
provider is for stopped-game review only. No experimental learning model
has been installed and no automatic category volume adjustment is enabled.
Pending clips retain the one-hour expiry and 2 GB save limit. Approved
exports remain permanent outside that temporary library. Choose export
folder changes the remembered destination.
