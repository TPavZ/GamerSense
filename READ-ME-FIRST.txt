GamerSense v0.3.1
Run BUILD-DEBUG.bat and launch src\GamerSense\bin\Debug\net9.0-windows\GamerSense.exe.

Weak matches now show Ambience / mixed audio. Reload matching is stricter.
Fallback does not verify that only ambience is present; it means no clear match.
Playback and remembered device IDs are unchanged. Matching needs 48 kHz stereo.

Developer checks: dotnet run --project tests/AnalyzerChecks/AnalyzerChecks.csproj
The model and rejection rules are bundled in src/GamerSense/Models.
validation contains corrected labels, retrospective rejection metrics, and
new-sample registrations. c1/c2/c3 are not trained or timestamp-reviewed yet.
See README.md for validation and limitations. Heuristic gates may miss true
reloads; they are not calibrated confidence or an independent accuracy estimate.
