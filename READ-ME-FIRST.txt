GamerSense v0.4.8 — lean shared output test

Close the old app, extract the ENTIRE app ZIP to a new folder, open GamerSense.exe.
Select Lean output (test) while stopped, then Start.
Compare delay and quality during 2-3 minutes of continuous audio.
Stop and Copy audio details, including LEAN OUTPUT and PLAYBACK SUPPLY.
If audio crackles, drops out, or fails to start, return to Event-driven capture
while stopped. Previous Event-driven, Minimum delay, and Stable modes remain.

Lean output targets one Windows device period (about 10 ms on C6), instead of
filling the allocated output capacity. It starts after 10 ms of captured audio.
Its requested 30 ms allocation is capacity/headroom, not queued-audio target.
Capture uses the clean 50 ms event-driven allocation; events deliver audio promptly.
Actual target, allocated capacity, padding, and silence fill are reported.
No claim of zero or measured end-to-end latency. Hardware audition is required.
Existing saved device and playback-mode choices are retained.

SOURCE: Build using BUILD-DEBUG.bat, then launch the built GamerSense.exe.

EVENT REVIEW: The latest 60 seconds are retained for reviewing spikes.
Mark with the button, Ctrl+Alt+F8 if available, or F8 within this app.
Select a marker, Load / refresh event clip, stop playback before Replay range.
Choose a label and Keep/Reduce/Unsure, then Save WAV + label. Keep both files.
Sound suppression is not implemented. Offline demo: Open audio sample > demo-vehicles.wav.
The black/teal/gold icon, spectrum and matching are retained.
