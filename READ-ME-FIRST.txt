GamerSense v0.4.13 - saved spike review

GET STARTED
Extract the whole ZIP into a new folder. App ZIP: open GamerSense.exe.
Source ZIP: run BUILD-DEBUG.bat, then open the Debug build executable.
The app requires .NET 9 Desktop Runtime; building requires .NET 9 SDK.

KEEP YOUR WORKING AUDIO SETTINGS
Mode: Direct cable capture (test)
Game audio device: CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)
True output: Speakers (C6)
Game output: CABLE Input
Windows Listen to this device: OFF
Your existing device selections and mode are remembered.

WHILE PLAYING
On Event review, leave Automatically save spikes and manual marks checked.
Spikes and level rises are saved with 2 seconds before and 3 seconds after.
Use Ctrl+Alt+F8 globally, or F8 while this app is focused, to mark an event.
You can keep playing without reviewing clips immediately.

AFTER THE GAME
1. Stop GamerSense playback.
2. Open Event review. Select a clip in the Pending list.
3. Use Replay range to hear it. Adjust the start/end range if useful.
4. Choose the sound tag and Keep, Reduce or Unsure, and add optional notes.
5. Choose Approve clip to keep it as reviewed, or Save tags to leave it Pending.
6. Choose Delete clip for an unwanted sample; deletion asks for confirmation.
7. Use Approved or All saved clips to return to reviewed clips.
8. Export WAV + label exports the selected range and a JSON label when needed.
   Pending saved clips remain unverified in exports.
Scroll the panel if the tag/approval buttons are below the visible area.

Clips remain available after Stop or an app restart until one hour after saving.
This includes Pending and Approved clips. Approval does not reset the timer.
Export useful clips before expiry if you want to keep them permanently. They are saved under %LOCALAPPDATA%/GamerSense/SavedEvents,
independently of the extracted app folder. Open saved folder shows them.
Do not move or rename library WAV/JSON pairs unless keeping your own backup.

STORAGE AND SAVING
Saved clips expire ONE HOUR after their original save time, including Approved
clips. Cleanup runs about every 30 seconds while the app is open, before saving,
and at startup. If the app is closed, expired clips are removed when reopened.
The audio storage budget is 2 GB. At the limit, new saves stop and a warning
appears; saving resumes when expiry or deletion makes room. Exported copies
are not part of automatic expiry. Do not use the library as permanent storage. At the current audio format,
a five-second clip uses about 1.9 MB. Busy sessions can create many clips.
The screen shows pending/approved counts, used space and unsaved/skipped clips.
Known analysis gaps or a slow/full disk can prevent an event from being saved.
Clips saved at Stop may have a shorter tail, identified in their list entry.
Allow a normal close to finish queued saves; forced termination may lose them.

Show live timeline and sample tools reveals the existing 60-second live view
and offline sample tools. Its expiry does not affect saved clips. Offline
sample events are not auto-saved; Save loaded clip for later saves the range.

Approvals and Keep/Reduce labels do not change audible playback or train the
model automatically. This update preserves v0.4.11 capture/output settings.
Please check audio remains clean during a normal game with autosave enabled.
