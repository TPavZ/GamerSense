Combined category similarity profiles from the 325-file private research collection.
4,515 eligible positive weak-label windows. Includes AC1 and AC2 reviewed WARDOGS clips,
real firearm/footstep examples, Sauerbraten Q009 effects, Stunt Rally effects
and WuxiaScrub helicopter SFX. Tentative suggestions only, not source separation
or confidence probabilities. Machine guesses are never approved labels.
The model has not been validated for accurate cross-game recognition or gains.
Creators/source notices and originals are retained in the learning packages.
